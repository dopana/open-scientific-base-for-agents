# Fundamentals of Organic Chemistry

## Overview

Organic chemistry is the study of carbon-containing compounds and their properties, structure, composition, reactions, and synthesis. Carbon's unique ability to form stable chains and rings makes it the backbone of biological molecules and synthetic materials.

## Key Concepts

### 1. Carbon Bonding

Carbon forms four covalent bonds, enabling diverse molecular architectures:

- **sp³ hybridization**: Tetrahedral geometry, single bonds
- **sp² hybridization**: Trigonal planar geometry, double bonds
- **sp hybridization**: Linear geometry, triple bonds

### 2. Functional Groups

Characteristic groups that determine chemical reactivity:

| Functional Group | Structure | Example |
|-----------------|-----------|---------|
| Alcohol | R-OH | Ethanol |
| Aldehyde | R-CHO | Formaldehyde |
| Ketone | R-CO-R' | Acetone |
| Carboxylic Acid | R-COOH | Acetic Acid |
| Amine | R-NH₂ | Methylamine |

### 3. Isomerism

Compounds with same formula but different structures:

- **Structural Isomers**: Different connectivity
- **Stereoisomers**: Same connectivity, different spatial arrangement
- **Enantiomers**: Non-superimposable mirror images (chirality)

## Reaction Types

### Substitution Reactions
One atom or group replaces another:

```
R-X + Y⁻ → R-Y + X⁻
```

### Addition Reactions
Atoms add across a multiple bond:

```
R-CH=CH₂ + H₂ → R-CH₂-CH₃
```

### Elimination Reactions
Atoms or groups are removed to form multiple bonds:

```
R-CH₂-CH₂-OH → R-CH=CH₂ + H₂O
```

## Importance

- **Pharmaceuticals**: Drug molecules are primarily organic compounds
- **Polymers**: Plastics, fibers, and elastomers
- **Biochemistry**: All life processes involve organic molecules
- **Materials Science**: Organic electronics, liquid crystals
